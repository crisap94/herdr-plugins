# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub's private reporting:
<https://github.com/crisap94/herdr-plugins/security/advisories/new>. Describe what happened and where. The
maintainer reviews every report and decides on a response, which may range from a private warning to a
permanent ban from the project's spaces. The maintainer will not share the identity of the reporter without
consent, except where the law requires it.
